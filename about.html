About Career Guidance Portal

Career Guidance Portal is designed to help students and learners understand their career options based on their education, interests and skills.

What is Career Guidance Portal?

Choosing the right career can be difficult, especially when there are many different career options available. Career Guidance Portal helps users explore suitable career paths by considering their educational qualification, interests and skills.


The portal provides a career recommendation along with information about the skills that may be useful, possible learning resources, a roadmap and an estimated timeline for developing those skills.

How Does It Work?

The portal follows a simple process to help users explore possible career paths.

1

Register

Create an account using your name, email and password.

3

4

Follow Roadmap

Use the suggested roadmap and resources to develop your career.

What Does the Portal Provide?

Career Matching

The system compares your profile with different career options and displays suitable matches.

Skill Guidance

Users can see important skills related to their recommended career and identify areas they can improve.

Career Roadmap

Each recommendation includes a step-by-step roadmap to help users understand what they can learn next.

Learning Resources

Suggested resources can help users find useful material for developing their knowledge and skills.

Estimated Timeline

Users can get an estimated timeline for developing the skills needed for their selected career path.

Beginner Friendly

The portal can also guide users who are just starting their career journey and may not have many skills yet.

Who Can Use This Portal?

The Career Guidance Portal can be useful for students and learners at different stages of education.


It can help users who have completed 10th, 12th, Diploma, Bachelor's or Master's level education explore possible career directions.


Users who are unsure about their career choice can use their interests and existing skills to explore different possibilities.

Important Note

Career recommendations are guidance

The career recommendations provided by this portal are intended to help users explore possible career options. A recommendation does not guarantee employment or admission to a particular course or career.


Users should also consider their personal goals, interests, financial situation, educational requirements and current opportunities before making an important career decision.
